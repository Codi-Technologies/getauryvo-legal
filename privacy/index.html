Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Auryvo collects, uses, shares, stores, and deletes information.

Effective date: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Auryvo LLC (“Auryvo,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Auryvo mobile application, website, and related services (the “Services”). This Policy explains the information we collect, how we use and disclose it, how long we retain it, and your choices. Auryvo is intended for adults 18 and older, with an initial public release intended for the United States. Contact: hello@getauryvo.com.

2. Information We Collect

Account information: email address, optional preferred name, account identifiers, authentication information, and records of eligibility acknowledgments, legal terms acceptance, and permissions.

Practice information: scenarios, goals, messages, AI responses, feedback, assessments, recaps, and saved content. You may choose to provide sensitive personal or professional details.

Coach memory: automatic capture is off by default and available to signed-in permanent-account users who separately opt in. Selected goals, preferences, challenges, and strategies may be used to personalize future practice. Turning automatic memory off stops new automatic saving and use of automatically saved memories; existing items remain available for review, editing, or removal. Memories you explicitly add remain separate from the automatic-memory setting. Sharing saved memory with OpenAI also requires AI permission.

Technical and usage information: structural events, usage and account identifiers, model and token usage, error codes, operational and security logs.

Purchases: Apple transaction identifiers, subscription status, entitlements, and dates. Apple processes payment; we do not receive complete payment-card numbers.

Notifications: preferences, reminders, and device push tokens where applicable. Support: your email address and information you send us.

The current app does not request contacts, precise location, photographs, or microphone access and does not currently include audio recording or speech recognition.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to create and secure accounts; provide AI simulations, coaching and feedback; store authorized practice history and coach memories; manage subscriptions, usage limits, and reminders; provide support; troubleshoot and maintain reliability; prevent misuse; and comply with law. We do not sell personal information or use private practice conversations for third-party advertising.

4. OpenAI and AI Consent

Auryvo uses OpenAI’s API. Depending on the feature, we may transmit practice setup, current and recent messages, optional preferred name, relevant saved coach memories, limited prior recaps, and a hashed account identifier. The simulated counterpart may receive less context than the coach. A hashed identifier is not necessarily anonymous.

We request separate, explicit permission before applicable personal information is sent for AI processing. You may withdraw permission using available app controls; AI features requiring that permission may stop functioning. Withdrawal does not reverse prior processing or automatically delete provider-held records.

Applicable OpenAI Responses API calls use store:false; this does not eliminate all provider operational or security retention. OpenAI states API content is not used to train its models by default unless a customer opts in. Auryvo has no separate model-training pipeline and does not intend to opt in. Under default arrangements, OpenAI may retain certain records for abuse monitoring generally up to 30 days, subject to exceptions and applicable settings.

5. Sharing

We share information as necessary with Supabase (authentication, database and backend functions, scheduled processing, configured auth email), OpenAI (AI processing), Apple (distribution, purchases and notifications), GitHub Pages (website hosting), and our support email provider. We may disclose information to comply with valid legal requests, prevent abuse, protect rights, or address disputes. We do not sell information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. The current website has no advertising trackers, analytics scripts, forms, or waitlist; hosting providers may keep technical logs.

6. Security

Safeguards include owner-scoped database controls, HTTPS, Supabase-managed encryption at rest, iOS Keychain for sessions, protected local files, and backend-managed credentials. Authorized systems may process readable practice content to provide the Services. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee against incidents.

7. Retention and Deletion

Deleted practices disappear from normal history immediately and are scheduled for permanent removal from active app records during the next daily purge after 30 days, together with linked messages, feedback, recaps, assessments, and practice-linked usage records. Related reminders are canceled and derived memories deactivated from active use. Deleting a practice does not remove memories you independently added; remove those separately using coach-memory controls or account deletion.

Removed coach memories stop being actively reused and are scheduled for permanent removal from active app records during the daily purge after 30 days. Earlier generated messages referring to removed memories are not automatically rewritten.

Provider backups, operational/security logs, support emails, Apple transactions, and legally retained records may have different schedules. The 30-day app deletion schedule does not guarantee deletion from all historical backups or independent third-party systems. Limited setup data, drafts, and pending work may remain in protected on-device files, subject to cleanup and device-backup behavior.

8. Account Deletion

You may initiate account deletion in the app. The configured process removes the authentication account and associated app database records, including coach memories. Historical backups and separate provider, support, security, or transaction records may remain as permitted by law. Deleting your account or uninstalling Auryvo does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions separately through Apple Account settings.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or copies of certain information and may have other rights concerning consent or processing. Use in-app controls for eligible practices, memories, notifications, and AI permission, or contact hello@getauryvo.com. We may verify requests and respond as required by law.

10. Third-Party Information and Age

Use care when discussing other people; do not submit confidential information without authorization, passwords, financial credentials, or government identification numbers. Auryvo is for adults 18 and older and uses an age self-attestation rather than date-of-birth or government-ID verification. We do not knowingly collect information from minors and will investigate and take appropriate deletion steps if notified.

11. International Processing and Changes

Auryvo LLC is based in Illinois. Providers may process information in the United States or elsewhere. The initial launch is intended for the United States. We may update this Policy with a revised effective date and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required.
