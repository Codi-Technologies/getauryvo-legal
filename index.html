Privacy by design

Your practice is personal.

Auryvo is designed to use the context needed for your rehearsal while keeping long-term coach memory under your control. The current implementation includes no advertising, cross-app tracking, email waitlist, or third-party marketing analytics.

You choose coach memory

Long-term memory is created for permanent accounts only when you add or approve an item. You can inspect, edit, and remove it.

AI context is bounded

Coaching uses the current practice, relevant approved memory, and up to three relevant saved recaps. Roleplay receives a narrower scene-only context.

Deletion controls matter

Saved practices can be deleted in the app and are scheduled for permanent removal after 30 days. Read the Privacy Policy for important limits.

Responsible use

Practice support, not professional advice.

AI can misunderstand context or produce inaccurate responses. You remain responsible for real-world decisions and conversations.

  • Not care. Auryvo is not therapy, medical care, mental-health treatment, or emergency support.
  • Not professional advice. It does not provide legal or financial advice.
  • Not a guarantee. A practice response or recap cannot predict how another person will respond.
